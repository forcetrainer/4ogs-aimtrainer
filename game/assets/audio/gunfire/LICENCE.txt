Gunfire in this folder is made from two recordings of a Carl Gustav M45
(a 9 mm submachine gun) in The Free Firearm Sound Library by Ben Jaszczak,
Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney, released under CC0 (no rights
reserved; no credit required).

Downloaded 2026-11-12 from
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
as "Prepared SFX Library.7z" (194 MB). Only source/G_31P.wav (close) and
source/G_20P.wav (mid distance) are kept here.

player_*.wav and enemy_*_azi_*.wav are cut and rendered from those two files
by tests/tools_make_gunfire.gd. Enemy shots use the SADIE II dummy-head
impulse responses in assets/hrtf (Apache-2.0).
